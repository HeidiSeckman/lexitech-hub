## 01 // Production Ecosystem

LexiTech Studio is an independent AI architecture and product lab building specialized platforms across forensic legal and tax intelligence, 24/7 voice-guided digital wellness, and customizable social networking.

### 1. LexiTech Legal & Tax AI (`v2.0`)
* **Launch:** [https://github.io](https://github.io)
* **Overview:** Zero-hallucination forensic contract and IRS intelligence suite engineered to audit agreements, detect tax collisions, and simulate crisis clauses.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Closed-book Legal & Tax Document AI anchored to **IRS Pub 4012 / 4491** exact section, tab, and page numbers.
  * Dual-Corpus Tax-Trap Scanner & automated redline suggestions.
  * Worst-Case Life Event Contract Simulator with **Lexi** neural voice synthesis.

### 2. LexiCare Sanctuary (`24/7 Live`)
* **Launch:** [https://streamlit.app](https://streamlit.app)
* **Overview:** Judgment-free twilight-lagoon AI companion offering unlimited conversational support, neural voice replies, and calming ambient soundscapes.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Dedicated **Vent**, **Problem-Solve**, and **Distract** conversational modes powered by **Ava** neural voice.
  * Built-in Rain, Ocean Waves, and Waterfall soundscape mixer.
  * 60-Second Calm Reset breathing guide and one-tap **988 / 741741 / 911** Safety Shield.

### 3. LeX Social (`13+ Live`)
* **Launch:** [https://streamlit.app](https://streamlit.app)
* **Overview:** Next-generation customizable social platform reviving classic profile expression with zero algorithms, modular drag-and-drop layouts, and 13+ `@handle` profiles.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * **LeX Studio Visual Customizer:** 20 dynamic SVG vector wall backgrounds, custom wallpaper upload, neon glowing borders, glass opacity/radius sliders, scrolling marquee tickers, and custom CSS overrides.
  * **Modular Drag-and-Drop Profile Layout:** Reorderable Profile Card, Photo Albums, Ranked Top 8 Friends, Profile Anthem & Audio-Only YouTube Playlists, and Profile Wall.
  * **Cloud Social Graph:** Unlimited followers, 1,000-friend cap, real-time feed with photo/link attachments, and two-way Direct Messages via Supabase.

## 02 // Core Studio Architecture

* **Grounded RAG & Closed-Book Verification:** Strict source-anchored extraction with zero unverified claims.
* **Real-Time Neural Audio:** Low-latency voice synthesis powered by ElevenLabs (`Lexi` & `Ava` Multilingual) and custom WebAudio/WAV synth engines.
* **Dynamic Model Resilience:** Automated multi-model Gemini Flash failover with session-only API key privacy and zero credential storage.
* **Crisis & Age-Gate Protection:** Built-in high-risk crisis escalation layers in LexiCare and strict `13+` date-of-birth enforcement on LeX Social.
